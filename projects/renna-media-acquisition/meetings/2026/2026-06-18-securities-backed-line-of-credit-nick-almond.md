## What It Is and How It Works

- Securities-backed line of credit (SBLOC): uses Fidelity brokerage accounts as collateral
- Revolving, interest-only: borrow and repay on any schedule, no draw or repayment periods
- No origination, application, annual, or early repayment fees: interest only
- Rate: SOFR (variable) plus a spread, currently ~6% to 6.5%
  - Moves mainly when the Fed moves; intra-cycle variance is a few basis points
  - Comparable to a HELOC rate, but lower fees and more flexibility
- Collateral call threshold: outstanding balance exceeds ~65% of pledged collateral
  - On $400K borrowed against $2.5M, would require a very severe drawdown
  - Three business days to cure: pay down the line or add collateral; bank notifies by email
  - If uncured, bank instructs Fidelity to liquidate enough to cover
