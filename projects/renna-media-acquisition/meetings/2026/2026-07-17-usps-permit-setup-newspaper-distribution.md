## USPS Account Setup

- Create a Business Customer Gateway account at gateway.usps.com
- This account generates a CRID number, critical to link to the new permit
- Also set up an Enterprise Payment Account within the gateway
- Form to file: PS Form 3615 (permit imprint application)
  - Annual permit fee: $390
  - Annual renewal fee: $390

## Visiting the Business Mail Entry Unit (BMEU)

Recommended next step: visit the BMEU in person. Not the regular post office counter, a separate unit often called the BMEU.

- Nearest location: 155 Clinton Road, Caldwell, NJ (approximately 20 minutes away)
- Bring the prior postage statement when visiting
- They can help with permit enrollment and linking to CRID, mail prep and sorting requirements, and electronic postage statement submission via the gateway

## Key Process Notes

- All postage statements must be submitted electronically via the Business Customer Gateway
- Permit number will be linked to the CRID from the new account
- Can call the USPS Mailing and Shipping Solutions Center again with further questions
