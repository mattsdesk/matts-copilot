## How the Relationship Works

No formal contracts, relationship-based, month-to-month. Joe's current workflow: page count Sunday evening, file upload Sunday night to Monday morning, 30 editions. Publication schedule: Joe proposes dates annually (usually November/December for the following year), targeting start of each month, with a February exception since the real estate awards issue sometimes pushes the date later.

Paper stock is primarily 30 lb newsprint (27.6 lb also in the mix due to supply tightness). The paper market has been volatile for about 9 months; prices settled high with no relief in sight. Jeremy commits to at least one month's notice on any pricing changes.

Inserts: Joe sends the insert order the Friday before the file-upload Sunday; Jeremy coordinates pickup from Doodad and stuffing.

Mailing: full saturation by zip code, no individual addresses, about 35 post offices across 30 editions. DDU entry used currently for a timeliness benefit; consolidation could save cost but is a future conversation. The carrier route list hasn't been updated in about 2 years; periodic updates recommended at small cost.

Color: Joe ran black and white by choice, not necessity. Jeremy confirmed color is a clear opportunity.
