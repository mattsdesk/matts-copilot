## Overall Diagnosis

- Too much text across the board: cut 50-60% minimum
- AI-generated subheadings everywhere, nobody reads them
- No visual variety: needs iconography, imagery, or contrast to break up the "blob"
- "Powered by Community Media" tagline confusing, remove it
- AI-added pill labels above sections: strip them all

## Homepage Messaging

- Core message buried: cheapest way to reach local audiences, print is persistent (not ephemeral like Facebook ads), easy with free design support
- Currently the meat ("30+ NJ towns, trusted local papers") is in the subheader, not the hero
- Proposed three-section structure:
  1. Best way to reach local customers (print vs. digital, coverage map)
  2. Cheapest option (side-by-side vs. Facebook ad cost)
  3. We make it easy (free design, done-for-you)
- Hero headline should lead with the key message, not "Your business delivered to 200,000 mailboxes"
- Interactive hover map (towns + publications) is a good long-term roadmap item
- CTA: "Explore products, starting at $100/month," then let them click through to pricing

## Pricing Page

- Current rate card is overwhelming; same problem as the old print flyer
- Proposed flow: reach first (towns/mailboxes), then size, then price updates in real time
  - Side-by-side comparison drove 7-10% conversion lift at Fubo
  - Size selector as a radio button or info icon within each card, not a separate module above
  - Labels like "Local Cluster" tell nobody anything: just say "3 towns, ~20K mailboxes"
- Option to simplify to print only, then upsell inserts/digital on the call
- Rate card grid stays in the newspapers themselves, not the website

## About Page and Site Architecture

- About page purpose: lend legitimacy, not tell the story
  - Lead above the fold with founding year (1998), newspaper logos, advertiser count
  - Add social proof: marquee advertiser names, gallery of strong ads
- Proposed IA: Home, Products/Pricing, Publications, About
  - Publications page is fine as-is, just a list
  - Kill or heavily trim anything that duplicates homepage modules

## Acquisition Context (Discussed Briefly)

- Targeting end-of-July close, ~6 weeks out
- Team assembled: broker, accountant, lawyer, all active on the contract
- Post-close growth angles: boots-on-ground sales vs. digital acquisition funnel, new ad products, hyper-local AI podcast, faceless YouTube channel, Nextdoor presence
- Renna Media as the marketing engine for future businesses, solving the "how do you get customers" problem
