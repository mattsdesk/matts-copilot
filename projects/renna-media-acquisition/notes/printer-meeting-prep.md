# Printer Meeting Prep - Engle Printing

**Context:** Joe's printer introduction, week of July 6 2026 (per 2026-06-23 touchpoint notes)
**Vendor:** Engle Printing and Publishing Company (black-and-white), plus a second color-insert vendor that delivers to Engle
**Why this matters:** No written printer contract exists. This is the single largest unconfirmed operational dependency ahead of the July 27 close and the August issue (goes to press ~July 26).

---

## Goal of the Conversation

Two things, in order:
1. Confirm the relationship survives ownership change on the same or better terms.
2. Start building your own relationship as the new counterparty, not just Joe's introduction.

Do not negotiate pricing today. Listen, confirm, build rapport.

---

## Questions to Ask

### Continuity and terms
- Is there a written contract or MSA with Renna Media, or has this always been handshake/PO-based?
- If handshake: are you open to formalizing terms once ownership transfers, or does that create friction?
- Will pricing, payment terms, and turnaround stay the same under Prospect Local Media LLC, or does a new customer setup trigger new rates?
- What's the current cost per unit, and has it moved in 2025-2026? Any increase planned?
- Payment terms today - net 15/30, deposit required, credit card?

### Production and capacity
- Walk me through the production calendar: when do you need final files, what's the print turnaround, when does it ship to the color-insert vendor and back?
- What's the latest a file can come in and still make the August issue (July 26 target)?
- What happens if a file is late - is there a grace window, rush fee, or does the issue slip?
- Current capacity vs. Renna's volume - how much headroom is there if page count or town count grows?

### Color insert vendor
- Who is the second vendor, and is that relationship yours (Engle manages it) or Joe's directly?
- Does that vendor need a separate introduction and account setup, or does it flow through you?
- Any risk if that vendor's costs or schedule shift?

### USPS permit and mailing
- Permit #1325 out of Lancaster, PA is reportedly in Joe's name personally, not the business. What's your understanding of that - is it tied to your facility, or is it Renna's account?
- Do you handle the mailing/addressing/sortation, or does that happen elsewhere?
- If the permit isn't transferable, what's involved in getting a new one set up under the new entity, and does that risk any delay to distribution?
- Any other accounts or numbers tied to Joe personally that I should know about (account rep, billing contact, login)?

### Backup and risk
- If Engle had a press issue or delay, is there a backup plan today?
- Are you aware of other publishers or newspapers you print for whose volume dips affect your pricing to Renna (i.e., is Renna's rate tied to shared press runs)?

### Relationship
- How long have you worked with Joe and Tina?
- Anything about Renna's production process that's been a recurring headache on your end?
- What do you need from me on day one to keep this seamless?

---

## What to Listen For

- Whether pricing/terms are informal enough that a new owner could get squeezed, or whether the relationship is stable and process-driven.
- Any hint that Joe personally (not the business) is the real counterparty - contract, permit, account, or otherwise.
- Whether the color-insert vendor is a second point of failure you haven't met yet.
- Tone: is this printer invested in Renna's success, or just a vendor filling a press slot?

---

## Exit With

1. Written or verbal confirmation pricing/terms hold post-close.
2. Clarity on the USPS permit - transferable, or new permit needed, and timeline either way.
3. Direct contact info for your day-one relationship (not routed through Joe).
4. A same-day debrief into `meetings/2026/2026-07-XX-angle-printing-intro.md` and an update to the printer/permit row in `analysis/diligence-questions.md`.
